How WSK works for partners

WSK aggregates user intent into meaningful demand, then connects that demand with partners who can deliver the experience.

1

Users express interest

"I want to try a beginner windsurf lesson on Saturday morning." A low-commitment signal, not a booking.

2

WSK identifies compatible demand

Users are grouped by activity, location, time slot and skill level into a group big enough to be worth running.

3

Relevant partners are offered the group

Partners serving that activity hear about it with demand already behind it. You see group size, level and timing. Individual details are shared only when a user chooses to book.

4

Partner hosts the session

You run the session, take payment and stay responsible for the experience. WSK earns a fee only if people attend.

How WSK earns

WSK is paid for participation, not exposure. We agree one of these with you before we send anyone, and you choose what suits your business.

🙋

Per attended participant

A fixed amount for each person we send who actually turns up. Works well if you already handle your own bookings and payments.

🧾

Commission on bookings

A percentage of the booking price for completed bookings. If a session is cancelled for weather, no fee is owed.

👥

Fee per filled group

A flat fee when WSK fills a session you offered. Useful while we are early and bookings are tracked by hand.
