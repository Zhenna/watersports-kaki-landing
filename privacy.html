WaterSports Kaki

Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

WaterSports Kaki ("WSK", "we") is an early-stage project based in Singapore. This page explains what personal data we collect through this website and our forms, why we collect it, who sees it, and how you can control it.

In short: we ask for your email and a few answers about what you'd like to try, so we can find sessions that fit you. Businesses only ever see anonymous counts such as "11 beginners want windsurfing on Saturday morning". We never share your name or contact details with a business without your say-so.

What we collect

  • Early-access form: your email; your name (optional); the sports you'd like to try; your level in each; when you're usually free; what would make watersports easier for you; anything else you choose to write.
  • Feedback form: your feedback and, only if you choose, your email and a description of who you are (for example a beginner).
  • Partner form: the business and contact details you enter.
  • Interest form for first-timers: when you'd prefer to learn (weekend, weekday or flexible); whether you've already tried to book a course; your email if you choose to give one; and your answer to whether we may pass your name and email to an instructor.
  • Website visits: aggregated page-view statistics, so we can see how many people visit. If you press Accept on our analytics notice, we also collect Google Analytics data: see Analytics below.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you when there is a session that fits what you told us.
  • To count how many people want each activity, level and time, so we can talk to operators about running sessions.
  • To count visits and see whether people come back, if you accept analytics.
  • To improve WaterSports Kaki.

Who sees your data

Operators and partners see only aggregate numbers. We do not sell personal data. We share individual details with a business only if you choose to book with them and agree to that.

Sessions we organise. If a session is organised, we email you to confirm a place. We pass your name and email to the instructor running it only if you answered Yes to being passed on, or after you confirm by email. We don't share your name or contact details in any other way.

We use these service providers to run the project: Google (Forms and Sheets, which store form responses, and Google Analytics if you accept it; Google may process data on servers outside Singapore) and Vercel (website hosting and page-view statistics).

Analytics

If you press Accept on our notice, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see whether people come back. It sets cookies in your browser (_ga and _ga_ followed by letters and numbers) and sends Google the pages you view, which features you use (for example checking conditions, opening Plan ahead, or clicking the registration or WhatsApp link), your type of device and browser, your approximate area, and where you came from. We don't send your name, email address or phone number. We use it to improve WaterSports Kaki and to show partners anonymised, combined counts of how many people are interested in an activity.

If you press Decline, or your browser sends Global Privacy Control, nothing is sent to Google Analytics. You can change your choice at any time: Analytics choices.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up answers while early access is running and for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. This includes your answers to the interest form for first-timers. After that we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time.

Google Analytics keeps its data for 14 months, then deletes it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can also withdraw your consent at any time, and change your analytics choice with the link in the Analytics section. Email lu.zhenna@watersportskaki.sg and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Age

WaterSports Kaki is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date above.
