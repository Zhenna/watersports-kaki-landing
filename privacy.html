WaterSports Kaki

Privacy notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

WaterSports Kaki ("WSK", "we") is an early-stage project based in Singapore. This page explains what personal data we collect through this website and our forms, why we collect it, who sees it, and how you can control it.

In short: we ask for your email and a few answers about what you'd like to try, so we can find sessions that fit you. Businesses only ever see anonymous counts such as "11 beginners want windsurfing on Saturday morning". We never share your name or contact details with a business without your say-so.

What we collect

  • Early-access form: your email; your name (optional); the sports you'd like to try; your level in each; when you're usually free; what would make watersports easier for you; anything else you choose to write.
  • Feedback form: your feedback and, only if you choose, your email and a description of who you are (for example a beginner).
  • Partner form: the business and contact details you enter.
  • Website visits: aggregated page-view statistics, so we can see how many people visit.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you when there is a session that fits what you told us.
  • To count how many people want each activity, level and time, so we can talk to operators about running sessions.
  • To improve WaterSports Kaki.

Who sees your data

Operators and partners see only aggregate numbers. We do not sell personal data. We share individual details with a business only if you choose to book with them and agree to that.

We use these service providers to run the project: Google (Forms and Sheets, which store form responses and may process data on servers outside Singapore) and Vercel (website hosting and page-view statistics).

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up answers while early access is running and for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. After that we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email lu.zhenna@watersportskaki.sg and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Age

WaterSports Kaki is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact
